# 📜 Legal Policies & Support Channels

Compliance, driver rights, and 24/7 technical assistance are central to the VINCY DRIVER ecosystem. The platform provides clear terms of service, robust data privacy policies, and direct communication channels with dispatch operators.

---

## 📞 Help Desk & Contact Channels (`ContactUsScreen`)

When encountering transit disruptions, ticketing disputes, or vehicle breakdowns, drivers reach dispatch immediately through multiple dedicated touchpoints:

| Support Channel | Integration | Functionality |
|-----------------|-------------|---------------|
| **Phone Hotline** | `url_launcher: tel:` | One-tap dialing to the Kingstown Central Transport Dispatch |
| **Email Support** | `url_launcher: mailto:` | Formal ticketing or payout query resolution |
| **Headquarters Map Pin** | `google_maps_flutter` | Interactive pin locating the physical operational office in Kingstown |

---

## 📜 Regulatory & Legal Documentation

### 1. About VINCY DRIVER (`/aboutUs`)
- Details platform mission to digitize public transit across Saint Vincent and the Grenadines.
- Highlights smart bus tracking, contactless QR ticketing, and transparent passenger manifests.

### 2. Terms & Conditions (`/termsAndCondition`)
- Sets standards for commercial transit operators.
- Governs driver payout cycles, cancellation deductibles, and code of conduct.
- Specifies vehicle roadworthiness obligations and safety compliance.

### 3. Privacy Policy (`/privacyPolicy`)
- Explains background GPS tracking disclosure and passenger privacy boundaries.
- Governs financial encryption, payment gateway compliance, and identity data retention.
